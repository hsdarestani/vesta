# Vesta V10 — Product + UX overhaul

Vesta is positioned as a **Daily Self-Care OS**, not a period tracker or generic habit app.

Core loop: **Check-in → Personalized day → Do → Track → Better personalization**.

Primary IA:
- **Today** — adaptive daily plan and 20-second check-in
- **Routines** — skin, hair, body and sleep routines
- **Vesta Guide** — context-aware daily guidance
- **Cycle** — cycle as personalization context, not the whole product
- **Me / Progress** — consistency, patterns and personal space

Secondary product surfaces:
- Beauty Cabinet
- Challenges
- Smart Shop
- Symptom logging

Visual direction:
- editorial, premium, mature and calm
- warm ivory, plum, dusty rose, sage and charcoal
- restrained gradients, orbital motifs and tactile product shapes
- no childish illustrations, no crowded dashboard UI, no excessive gamification

Product rule: every major surface should answer one of these questions:
1. What should I do today?
2. Why is this right for me today?
3. What am I learning about myself over time?
